Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join a workshop?
Click "Register Now" and you'll be redirected to WhatsApp. Our team will confirm your registration and send you the Zoom link before the session.
Will I get a recording?
Yes! All registered attendees receive a recording of the full session within 24 hours of the workshop ending.
Are workshops in Hindi or English?
Most workshops are conducted in Hinglish (a mix of Hindi and English) to make them accessible and comfortable for all attendees.
Can I attend from outside India?
Absolutely! Our Zoom workshops are open to participants worldwide. Times are listed in IST but recordings are available for all time zones.
How do I pay for paid workshops?
Payment details are shared via WhatsApp after registration. We accept UPI, bank transfer, and credit/debit cards.
