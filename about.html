Our Journey

From Vision to Impact

2012
Nivam Is Founded

Started as a one-on-one coaching practice in Mumbai, with a focus on personal growth and family wellness.

2015
First Group Program Launched

Parvarish debuted as our first structured group coaching program, enrolling 40 families in the first cohort.

2018
National Recognition

Awarded Best Family Coaching Organisation at the India Wellness Summit. Expanded to Delhi and Pune.

2020
Going Online

Launched live Zoom workshops and digital programs, reaching families across India and the globe.

2025
5,000+ Lives Transformed

Today, Nivam has touched over 5,000 lives with 6 signature programs, 200+ workshops and a thriving community.
